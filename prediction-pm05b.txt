exact trusted image hashes/types/modes/owners/link topology, no payload rewrite,
and refusal on local changes. Boot/kernel/passwords/homes remain image-owned or
user-owned. A full image inventory cannot be imported as F5: boot/var paths and
set-ID bits are prohibited, and generated files differ per installation.
